## My notes

Semantic search worked best for Q1, where the top score was 0.756 and
two of the top 3 chunks contained the group size rule. It also found the
single-defaulter rule for Q3 and the parallel production chunk for Q8,
although that one was at rank 2. It failed for Q10: my FAQ page says PGS
products cannot be exported as organic, but none of the top 3 chunks
mentioned export. For Q2 and Q5 the "relevant" marks from my word check were
misleading, because the chunks only contained the same words (for example the
title page mentions Natural Farming) without answering the question, so I
corrected them by reading the chunks. Q6 (the Pune shop) scored 0.518, which
is higher than the scores of Q7 (0.465) and Q8 (0.437), two questions my data
can answer. So a score limit alone cannot say "I don't know", and I need the
language model to refuse when the chunks do not contain the answer.
